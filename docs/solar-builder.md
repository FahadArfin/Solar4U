# Guided solar shopping

Open **Build a system** (`/solar-part-picker`, also `/build`). The builder shares the equipment notebook's device-local document. Existing notebooks remain intact at `/equipment`.

Choose a slot in the compact parts table, narrow its subtype, filter current observations, and compare up to four exact retailer variants. Adding from browsing or comparison returns to the build. Edit purchased quantities directly in the table (Enter or blur saves); invalid values show an error without changing storage. Replace opens the same category and keeps the original until a new variant saves. The replacement retains row identity and notes, requires an explicit new purchase quantity, and rejects concurrent changes to the original row. Quantity counts purchased variants or packages, not automatically individual modules. Adding stores the observed price and timestamp; subsequent price changes do not silently alter a saved budget. Remove supports undo. Use the notebook to edit notes and categories, import a backup, add a manual quote, or explicitly accept an updated price. Backup exports a restorable parts JSON; Shopping list exports CSV with retailer links and formula-injection protection.

The workbench supports renaming the shared parts list, four project-purpose guidance choices, a roof/ground illustration, and a budget target. Preferences use a separate validated device-local document, `solar4u-builder-preferences-v1`; they are not included in the parts backup. The illustration is symbolic, not a measured design or electrical connection diagram. Highlighted categories mean selected equipment only. All six shopping categories stay available regardless of purpose. Budget targets track one currency without conversion; changing the budget currency clears the target. Unpriced items are excluded and disclosed. A zero target is distinct from no target. The browser previews each candidate's effect on that currency's saved subtotal, including removal of the old row during replacement.

Slots include panels, ground/roof mounting and accessories, electrical boxes/switches, wiring/fuses, inverters/controllers, and batteries. Battery format and voltage class have independent filters. Combined kits, empty enclosures and cells have distinct categories. Optional categories do not create a misleading completion percentage.

Classification and numeric clues come from retailer titles and selected variants, not verified manufacturer specifications. Selected variants override ambiguous family values. Formatted numbers and ranges are handled conservatively. Module price per stated watt requires an explicit package quantity and an unambiguous module wattage; kits containing controllers or mounting are excluded. Shared SKUs surface potential alternatives in the same category and currency, without claiming verified equivalence. Check exact contents and datasheets.

Known-price subtotals use integer minor units and keep currencies separate. Shipping, freight and tax are not quoted. Compatibility requires missing manufacturer and site inputs: cold Voc/hot Vmp and controller limits, battery/BMS support, structural mounting approval, and AC/DC protection ratings. No universal compatibility pass is generated.

Daily collection is bounded discovery plus rotation of known variants, not every product on the internet. Source coverage is visible, with honest empty states for missing accessories. Manual items and quotes remain available. No purchase or checkout is initiated by adding a component.

Native WebMCP tools expose the current build, filtered offers, category navigation and exact-offer quantity additions. `solar4u_set_build_quantity` edits an existing row through the same validated, locked save path as the visible field. Native additions always add; they do not silently use an open replacement flow. Reading offers does not expose an account or complete a purchase.

## September 12 interaction audit and verification

The prior live builder used a large hero and tall category cards; the first viewport barely reached the first component, and quantity edits left the builder for the notebook. The PCPartPicker list and CPU selection flow were captured in the browser as the structural reference. The redesign adopts its compact component/selection/quantity/price working table and choose-compare-return loop while preserving Solar4U's cream and sage palette and honest solar-specific uncertainty.

Browser checks exercised comparison, adding, replacement with a different package quantity, reload persistence, zero-quantity rejection, remove/undo, budget arithmetic, and native quantity changes. At a 390px viewport the selected-part table reflows into cards without page overflow. These checks cover the builder flow, not a full-site accessibility certification. Unit regressions cover replacement identity, notes, unrelated rows, concurrent changes, deleted rows, invalid quantities, and budget validation.
